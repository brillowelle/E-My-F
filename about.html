About Us

Eat My Fry is a globally inspired, 100% Halal Quick Service Restaurant built on quality, compassion, and modern flavours — now reimagined with a Saudi touch for a richer, more culturally rooted dining experience. Our brand was created to make great food accessible to every community, combining premium quality, health-conscious recipes, and authentic hospitality drawn from Saudi warmth and generosity.

From crispy fried chicken, wings, burgers, wraps, rice bowls, pastas, and more, every dish is crafted using fresh ingredients and carefully developed recipes. We blend global fast-food excellence with a Saudi-styled dining spirit, where flavours are bold, service is heartfelt, and every meal feels like it’s made for family.

Beyond food, we carry a strong humanitarian purpose — supporting children in Gaza and Africa through monthly contributions and our “Buy 1 Share 1” initiative. With Eat My Fry, every bite creates an impact. Rooted in quality, shaped by community, and flavoured with a touch of Saudi heritage, we are here to redefine what modern Halal QSR can be.

Core Values

Our Core Values

At Eat My Fry, our values are the heart of everything we do. They guide our decisions, shape our culture, and define our promise to you.

01

Quality First — Fresh, Honest, and Uncompromised

We are committed to serving only the freshest, cleanest, and finest Halal ingredients. Every meal reflects our dedication to premium quality and Saudi-inspired culinary standards — ensuring flavour, freshness, and trust in every bite.

02

Customer-Centric

Your satisfaction is at the core of our brand. We listen, adapt, and serve with warmth, generosity, and respect — values deeply rooted in Saudi culture. Every visit is crafted to feel memorable, welcoming, and genuinely cared for.

03

Innovation — Bold Ideas, Better Experiences

We continuously push boundaries, whether in taste, technology, or service. From new Saudi-styled flavours to modern QSR systems, our mission is to elevate fast food into a forward-thinking, dynamic experience built around your needs.

04

Inclusivity — Great Food for Every Community

From cities to small towns, families to students, and locals to expats, we believe everyone deserves access to delicious food and meaningful dining experiences. Our brand is built on openness, diversity, and the universal joy of sharing a meal.

Our Mission & Vision

Mission

Our mission is simple yet impactful: to make people happy by serving delicious, high-quality food in a welcoming ambience that’s accessible to everyone, everywhere. We strive to redefine fast food with healthier choices, exceptional flavors, and a memorable dining experience for every guest who walks through our doors. Our commitment extends beyond food—we aim to build a community where every meal brings people together with joy and satisfaction

Vision

Our vision is to become World’s most loved QSR brand, known for our commitment to quality, health, and innovation. We’re not just serving meals; we’re creating experiences that bring people together. Through our diverse menu, sustainable practices, and customer-first approach, we aim to set a new standard in the fast-food industry
